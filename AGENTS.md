# Project Architecture Rules
- Auth hydration must never bounce a direct URL load: ProtectedRoute and DashboardLayout wait on `rolesReady` from useAuth, and the legacy sessionStorage `authenticated` flag is written as soon as any session exists (not after roles load), so late role hydration can't redirect staff to the dashboard/login.

- Reports paginate date-scoped financial and completed-ticket queries, including the comparison period and older-intake completions; interpret picker days in Manila so totals are complete across browser timezones.

- Public quote response eligibility lives in a tested helper; reopened declines may respond only while Waiting to Proceed and unlocked, preserving completed approvals and partial-approval locks.

- Keep home-screen install metadata and OneSignal push, but do not use an app-shell service worker; stale offline shells caused recurring blank screens after releases.
- Store daily staff-duty acknowledgements in activity logs keyed by account and Manila date so dismissal follows the staff account across devices without a second state table.- Public /track reads tickets only through PIN-verified database functions (last 4 phone digits); the unverified snapshot is staff-only, so the check cannot be bypassed in the browser.
- Intake auto-assignment uses a daily round-robin (fewest tickets today, then longest since last assignment, then day-rotated roster); use the shared staff availability check for eligibility so intake and ticket-edit screens follow the same attendance rule.
- Inventory staff = admin role + extra 'inventory' role row; stock changes are captured by database triggers into stock_movements (auto-approved for inventory/management), so every code path that edits stock is audited without client changes.
- On Hold is the `services.is_on_hold` toggle layered over the real status (use `isOnHold` from serviceStatus); the legacy "On Hold" status is display-only so paused tickets resume at their own status.
- Interim decline transitions use the tested function-local helper; proposed RTO holds use a status-free update so another staff member's latest repair stage is preserved.
- Interim reports are versioned in `interim_reports` (one pending round at a time, rules in `src/lib/interimRounds.ts`); `services.interim_*` mirrors the open round so the existing approval flow keeps working.
- Delivery staff have a single `delivery` role row and are confined to /delivery by ProtectedRoute; pickup entries live in `pickup_schedules`, and zone/day rules live in `src/lib/pickupZones.ts`.
- Company devices are intaken through the normal intake form or the multi-device Company intake dialog, which sets device type/department/technician per device via the tested `planCompanyAssignments` (same round-robin + availability rules); Company Repairs groups normal tickets via `services.company_id`/`company_batch_id` (each device stays its own ticket for Manage Client/Service Update); quotations store their payload in `company_quotations` and the PDF is regenerated on demand with the shared pdfPremiumKit letterhead, and the public `/company/:token` page reads only through `public_company_portal`.
- Company quotation previews share QuotationPreviewButton and PdfViewerModal, rendering the regenerated PDF as page images so staff and clients can view every page on mobile without iframe PDF limitations.
- Inventory stock status is derived, not typed: `deriveStockStatus` (src/lib/stockStatus.ts) gives Out of Stock at 0, Low Stock at/below `reorder_level` (0 = no warning), else In Stock; only "On Order"/"For Ordering" are kept as saved. useInventory reads every part past the 1,000-row API cap and returns the derived status, so screens never re-derive low stock on their own.
- Ticket `parts_used` lines are always `PART-ID (qty)` (src/lib/partsStock.ts) from both the Parts Used panel and Service Update; saving warns before taking more than is in stock (only parts added since the last save count).
- Inventory CSV import (src/lib/inventoryImport.ts + InventoryImportDialog) matches parts by SKU = `part_id`, reads the old Google Sheet's Inventory Dashboard CSV or the app's own CSV download, previews before saving, never deletes, and is limited to inventory/management so its stock movements auto-approve.
- Adjust Stock sends the bridge only `add`/`subtract` (the bridge treats anything else as add): Remove becomes subtract and Manual Adjustment sends the difference to the entered count, both worked out in InventoryManagement before posting.
- The "How to use" side panels on Inventory and Company Repairs (PageGuide) read their steps from src/lib/pageGuides.ts; when a button, tab or field is renamed, update the guide text too.
- The parts list (InventoryItemsTable: table on md+, cards on phones, row details on click) is shared by Inventory Management and the read-only Check Parts page (/parts-availability, menu for admin and technician; no cost, no stock actions). Inventory's Assign to Ticket reuses PartsUsedPanel, so stock deduction, shortfall warnings and logs match the ticket screens.
